# 🌸 BloomHer

### Women Wellness & Lifestyle Platform

BloomHer is a modern and responsive **Women Wellness Website** designed to support physical, mental, and emotional well-being in one place.

The platform provides a simple and calming experience where women can track daily wellness activities, explore fitness and nutrition guidance, monitor mood and menstrual cycles, and access supportive wellness resources.

## ✨ Features

* 🌸 **Personalized Dashboard**
  View and manage daily wellness activities in one place.

* 😊 **Mood Tracking**
  Track daily moods and understand emotional well-being over time.

* 💧 **Water Intake Tracking**
  Monitor daily water consumption and build healthy hydration habits.

* 😴 **Sleep Tracking**
  Keep track of sleep patterns and encourage better sleep habits.

* 🌙 **Menstrual Cycle Tracking**
  Track menstrual cycles and maintain personal wellness records.

* 🧘 **Fitness & Yoga Guidance**
  Explore simple fitness and yoga activities for a healthier lifestyle.

* 🥗 **Nutrition & Healthy Diet**
  Get healthy food and nutrition suggestions to support everyday wellness.

* 🧠 **Mental Wellness Support**
  Provides resources and supportive tools for maintaining mental well-being.

* 🤖 **AI-Powered Assistant**
  Provides personalized wellness recommendations and assistance.

* 👩‍🤝‍👩 **Community Support**
  Encourages connection and support around women's wellness.

## 🎨 Design

BloomHer focuses on creating a **calming, simple, and user-friendly interface**.

The design is built to provide:

* Easy navigation
